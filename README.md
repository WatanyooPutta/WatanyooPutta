## 🔬 Research & Innovation Highlights
* **Machine Learning & Snake Venom Biosensors:** Winner of the Minimal Invasive Medical Device Competition organized by the National Research Council of Thailand (NRCT). Developed an electrochemical biosensor integrated with machine learning for rapid point-of-care venom detection.
* **Environmental Health Publications:** Authored an Independent Study investigating construction-generated noise impacts on student well-being. Published openly on Zenodo: [DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23219819](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23219819).
* **Tropical Disease Research:** Conducted research on *Opisthorchis viverrini* (Southeast Asian liver fluke) utilizing cell culture, gel electrophoresis, and Western blot techniques at Khon Kaen University's Tropical Disease Research Centre.

## 📡 Satellite Telemetry & Tech
* **Amateur Radio (Callsign: E25ZVB):** Licensed Basic Amateur Radio Operator.
* **TinyGS Ground Station:** Operating the 433 MHz `SatFinder_KKN_KKW` node to receive and distribute live CubeSat telemetry to a global open-source network.

## ⚕️ Clinical Volunteering
Active contributor to prehospital emergency response and community welfare. Social Worker under the Ministry of Social Development and Human Security (MSDHS) and coordinator for blood/organ donation initiatives with the Thai Red Cross Society.
